### Why HushJot

- **Instant.** 0.5 ms per keystroke, typing 434 ms after a cold start.
- **Private.** Encrypted in your browser before it goes anywhere. We can't read your notes.
- **Anywhere.** Any browser, a locked-down work laptop included. Installs to your phone and works offline.
- **Built for text and code.** Live preview, ~150 highlighted languages, Mermaid, tables, task lists, up to four notes side by side.
- **Fast search.** 10,000 encrypted notes searched on your device in 18 ms.

### Your coding agent's remote control

Plan on your phone; your agent builds at your desk. Write what you want in a note, answer its questions, tick Approve, and it reports back in your Inbox.

```bash
npm install -g @hushjot/cli
hushjot login
```

Tested with Claude Code. Setup guides for Codex, Copilot, Gemini, Grok and Cursor. [How it works →](https://hushjot.com/agents/)
